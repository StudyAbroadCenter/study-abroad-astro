# Institution Logo Policy

## Purpose

The Global Learning Network may show the names of universities and institutions with which participation or Custom Program activity has been verified.

Institution names, participation counts, and Custom Program labels are evidence data. University logos are separate trademarked brand assets and must not be treated as evidence data.

## Production rule

Use an institution logo on the public Ritsumeikan Short-Term Programs site only when the site operator can demonstrate that the logo may be used for this external web context.

Finding a logo on an official university website, brand guide, media kit, or download page does **not** by itself establish third-party usage permission.

If permission is unclear, use the neutral initials fallback.

## Never use

- Google or browser favicons as university logos
- third-party logo aggregation services
- guessed logo URLs based on domains
- AI-generated or redrawn university marks
- unofficial student organisation marks
- faculty or departmental marks when the evidence refers to the university as a whole
- altered colours, proportions, crops, or compositions of official marks

## Registry states

### 1. Approved for this site

The operator has documented permission, licence, or an applicable official usage rule that permits this external use.

Only these institutions may be added to `approvedLogos` in `GlobalNetworkInstitutionEnhancer.astro`.

### 2. Approval required

An official asset exists, but the university's published rules require prior approval for external/non-university use, or otherwise limit third-party use.

Keep initials until approval is documented.

Examples identified during review include institutions whose official brand rules expressly require approval for external logo use.

### 3. Permission unconfirmed

An official UI/logo asset is publicly visible or downloadable, but the published material reviewed does not clearly establish that a third party may use it on this site.

Keep initials until the permission position is clear.

## Evidence and logo data must remain separate

- Participation numbers come from the verified 2023–2026 RSJP/RWJP integrated dataset.
- `CUSTOM` indicates separately verified Custom Program activity.
- A logo must never be used to imply that an institution is formally endorsing the website or a programme.
- A missing logo does not mean the institutional relationship is weaker or unverified; it only means logo usage rights have not been cleared.

## Review checklist before adding a logo

1. Confirm the institution name matches the evidence dataset.
2. Use the institution's official website or official brand/identity documentation as the primary source.
3. Check specifically for external-use, partnership, co-branding, trademark, or permissions rules.
4. Confirm the exact asset is the university-level mark intended for digital/web use.
5. Preserve aspect ratio, colour, clear space, and minimum-size guidance.
6. Record the source and permission basis.
7. If any point remains unclear, keep initials.

## Design fallback

The initials fallback is a deliberate production state, not an error state. It is designed to keep the Global Learning Network complete, visually consistent, and legally conservative while permissions are being confirmed.
