# RWJP Photo Library

Updated: 2026-08-15

## Purpose

This file records the real-photo assets selected for the RWJP recruitment page and the wider candidate pool supplied for future reuse.

The goal is to keep photography reusable and auditable without treating every photograph as evidence of a specific 2027 RWJP session.

## Editorial rules

- Use real programme / international-education photography where it supports the applicant journey.
- Do not imply that a photographed activity is a confirmed 2027 RWJP activity unless that fact is independently verified in the canonical programme data.
- Prefer photographs in which faces are not the primary visual subject.
- For public-web use, crop for context and apply restrained privacy softening where individual identification would otherwise dominate the image.
- Avoid mosaic-style anonymisation, synthetic replacement of people, or visual effects that make an authentic photograph look artificial.
- Keep colour correction natural: exposure, white balance and contrast may be adjusted, but the scene itself should remain documentary in character.
- Web delivery assets should normally be WebP and sized for the actual display context.

## Privacy note

The public-web derivatives use restrained facial-detail softening to reduce individual identifiability while preserving the atmosphere of the original photographs. This is a presentation safeguard, not a substitute for any institutional consent / image-use policy that may apply to publication.
