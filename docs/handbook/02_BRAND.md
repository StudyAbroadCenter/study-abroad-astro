# RSJP Experience System — Brand Framework

## Brand Purpose

Make the experience of studying through a Ritsumeikan short-term programme understandable, credible, and emotionally tangible before the student arrives.

## Brand Promise

Offer a structured, welcoming, academically meaningful way to experience Japan through Ritsumeikan University.

Never promise an experience the programme cannot reliably deliver.

## Position

The brand sits between:

- university education,
- study abroad,
- Japanese language learning,
- cultural experience,
- student life,
- international exchange.

It should be:

- more human than a corporate university site,
- more academically credible than a tourism site,
- more mature than a language-school advertisement,
- more welcoming than an application portal.

## Core Brand Idea

**Study Japan by living it.**

The programme connects academic learning with lived experience.

## Brand Values

### Authenticity
Show real programme life.

### Trust
Use accurate facts, transparent conditions, and professional consistency.

### Welcome
Communicate that international students are expected and supported.

### Curiosity
Encourage exploration of language, culture, society, people, and places.

### Growth
Present opportunities for development without guaranteeing transformation.

### Connection
Emphasise real opportunities for interaction with students, teachers, communities, and peers.

## Personality

The brand is:

- knowledgeable,
- welcoming,
- calm,
- curious,
- international,
- confident,
- thoughtful,
- approachable.

It is not:

- loud,
- bureaucratic,
- childish,
- sales-driven,
- self-important.

## Voice

Explain rather than announce.

Invite rather than sell.

Inform rather than impress.

## Emotional Sequence

Trust → Curiosity → Anticipation.

Do not create excitement first and explain credibility later.

## Institutional Relationship

Ritsumeikan University provides:

- academic credibility,
- institutional legitimacy,
- campus identity,
- trust.

The recruitment experience provides:

- accessibility,
- student perspective,
- experience,
- personality.

The site should feel like Ritsumeikan for prospective international students, not a miniature copy of a corporate site.

## Academic Credibility

Do not present programmes as extended tourism.

Show academic credibility through real academic elements where they exist.

Do not equate academic credibility with bureaucratic tone.

## Japan

Present Japan as a real place to study and live.

Avoid:

- exoticisation,
- mystification,
- stereotypes,
- decorative cultural clichés.

Show tradition and contemporary life together.

## Kyoto / Osaka / Other Locations

Each location should retain its own character.

Do not force a Kyoto aesthetic or narrative onto programmes elsewhere.

## Students

Students are participants, not marketing props.

Prefer imagery and stories that show them:

- studying,
- talking,
- moving,
- participating,
- interacting.

## Premium

Premium means:

- carefully considered,
- restrained,
- polished,
- consistent,
- trustworthy.

It does not mean luxury.

## Differentiation

Differentiate through:

- authenticity,
- real university experience,
- meaningful access to Japan,
- interaction,
- transparent information,
- high-quality digital experience.

Do not compete primarily through slogans or effects.

## Marketing

Good marketing helps the right student decide to apply and helps unsuitable applicants decide not to.

Recruitment quality matters more than raw application volume.

## Transparency

Do not hide:

- costs,
- restrictions,
- application requirements,
- accommodation conditions,
- status,
- limitations.

Transparency strengthens the brand.

## Brand Decision Test

Before approving a brand decision:

1. Is it true?
2. Is it useful to students?
3. Is it authentic?
4. Does it strengthen trust?
5. Does it reflect the actual programme?
6. Does it reduce unnecessary institutional complexity?
7. Could any generic study-abroad site say the same thing?

If question 7 is yes, make it more specific.

## Priority

1. Truth
2. Trust
3. Student relevance
4. Clarity
5. Authenticity
6. Accessibility
7. Emotional appeal
8. Visual distinction
9. Marketing impact
