# Editorial Easter Eggs

## Purpose

The Japanese site may contain a small number of playful cultural references. Their purpose is to reward attentive readers with a quiet smile, not to turn the university site into a collection of memes.

Every line must still work as clear programme copy for a reader who does not know the reference.

## Rules

1. Do not reproduce song lyrics, catchphrases or titles as quotations.
2. Do not name the source artist, performer or programme on the public page.
3. Transform the rhythm or structure into original language about learning and exchange.
4. Use no more than one noticeable reference in the same viewport.
5. Keep formal information—dates, fees, eligibility, safety and cancellation rules—free from jokes.
6. Do not force Japanese references into the English, Korean or Chinese editions. Each language needs its own culturally natural voice.
7. Remove a reference if it weakens trust, accessibility or clarity.

## Current Japanese microcopy

### Programme Finder

> あなたは、何が好き？ 日本語。京都。人との出会い。それとも、まだ知らないテーマ。

This works as a genuine discovery prompt while lightly echoing a familiar call-and-response rhythm.

### RSJP Express

> 2週間でも、イイじゃん。学びも、出会いも、可能性も。倍々に。

This supports the real message that a shorter programme is not a lesser experience.

### Student co-learning

> 交流すんのかい？ します。しかも、一緒に学びます。

The joke immediately returns to the academic distinction between social exchange and co-learning.

### Cultural Kyoto

> 京都、きれいなだけでは終わらない。

This reframes Kyoto as a place of context, practice and reflection rather than a decorative tourist setting.

### Learning beyond the classroom

> 書を持って、教室を出よう。

This is an original adaptation used to express the programme philosophy: the city and society are part of the classroom.

## Review test

Before publishing any new reference, ask:

- Does the sentence still make sense without knowing the source?
- Does it support the programme's educational value?
- Is it quieter than the main message?
- Would a student smile without a university partner losing confidence?

If any answer is no, remove it.
