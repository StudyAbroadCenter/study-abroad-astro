# Photo upload guide

Upload original photographs into the most suitable folder below. Keep the original files whenever possible; web crops and optimized copies can be prepared later.

## Main folders

- `hero/` — strong landscape photographs for the top page
- `campus/oic/`, `campus/kinugasa/`, `campus/bkc/` — campus buildings and scenery
- `japanese-class/` — Japanese language classes and learning scenes
- `japan-studies/` — tea ceremony, taiko, kimono, calligraphy, sports and field trips
- `student-life/` — buddies, orientation, cafeteria, library and general campus life
- `accommodation/` — residence halls and rooms
- `kyoto/` — temples, nature and city scenes
- `programs/` — photographs specific to RSJP, RSJP Express, RWJP, RWJP Express, RDSP, RBMP and custom programs
- `misc/` — photographs that are difficult to classify

## File naming

Descriptive English file names are preferred when practical, for example:

- `rsjp-japanese-class-01.jpg`
- `rwjp-tea-ceremony-02.jpg`
- `oic-campus-spring-01.jpg`
- `buddy-activity-01.jpg`

Original camera names such as `IMG_1234.JPG` are also acceptable at the upload stage.

## Privacy and publication

Do not upload photographs containing confidential documents, visible personal information or images that must not be published. Photographs with people may later be cropped, darkened or blurred where appropriate, but publication rights should still be checked before final release.
