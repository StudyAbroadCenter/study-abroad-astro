# RSJP Experience System — Product Direction

## Purpose

This is not a university information portal.

It is a student-centred recruitment experience that helps prospective international students understand short-term programmes, trust the information, imagine participating, and confidently decide what to do next.

## Primary Audience

Prospective international university students, many of whom may:

- be considering short-term study in Japan for the first time,
- use English as a second or additional language,
- compare several universities or programmes,
- browse mainly on mobile,
- have questions about cost, accommodation, language ability, safety, or application procedures.

Secondary users include parents, partner universities, academic staff, and study-abroad advisors.

Student experience remains the primary design priority.

## Product Goals

The site should help users:

1. discover the right programme,
2. understand what they will study and experience,
3. find essential facts quickly,
4. evaluate suitability,
5. trust the organiser,
6. proceed to application without confusion.

## Non-Goals

The project is not intended to:

- reproduce the university corporate website,
- become a general university portal,
- publish every internal administrative detail,
- optimise primarily for staff organisational structure,
- introduce enterprise architecture during MVP,
- introduce a CMS before it is operationally justified,
- maximise effects or animation at the expense of clarity and speed.

## MVP

Focus on:

- programme discovery,
- programme details,
- dates,
- fees,
- eligibility,
- accommodation,
- programme experience,
- campus/location,
- FAQ,
- application guidance,
- clear calls to action.

## Trust

Accuracy has priority over persuasion.

Do not hide limitations, restrictions, costs, or conditions.

## Technology Direction

Current preferred direction:

- Astro
- TypeScript
- GitHub
- Vercel

Use the existing styling approach in the repository.

Do not introduce a CSS framework or replace the styling system without a documented project-level reason.

## Decision Priority

1. Accuracy and trust
2. Student experience
3. Clarity
4. Accessibility
5. Performance
6. Maintainability
7. Visual polish
8. Development convenience
