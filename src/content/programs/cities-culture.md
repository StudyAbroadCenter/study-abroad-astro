## About the program

This interdisciplinary program uses a fictional Japanese city as a living classroom. Morning seminars introduce ideas in urban planning and cultural continuity, while guided site visits invite students to consider how communities respond to change.

## What you will learn

- Compare approaches to urban development and neighborhood identity.
- Practice respectful observation and field-note methods.
- Work in an international team on a community-focused presentation.

## Sample learning format

The schedule combines small-group seminars, facilitated excursions, independent reflection and a final collaborative project. All teaching in this prototype program is described as taking place in English.
