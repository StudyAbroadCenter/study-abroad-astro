## About the program

Island Sustainability Lab is a fictional, place-based summer course focused on the links between environment, livelihoods and visitor economies. Students examine case studies and develop practical ideas with peers.

## What you will learn

- Map the stakeholders involved in a local sustainability challenge.
- Evaluate environmental initiatives from multiple perspectives.
- Communicate a realistic proposal to a general audience.

## Sample learning format

Workshops and coastal field visits are balanced with structured team time. The course concludes with a public-style showcase where each group explains its prototype solution.
