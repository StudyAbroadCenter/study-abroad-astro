## About the program

This fictional winter program brings introductory language study together with observation-led design. Students practice useful communication and investigate how everyday experiences are shaped for clarity, care and convenience.

## What you will learn

- Use beginner-level Japanese in common daily situations.
- Apply observation and interview frameworks to a design question.
- Reflect on assumptions in intercultural communication.

## Sample learning format

Interactive language classes are followed by studio sessions, local visits and conversation practice. No prior study of Japanese is assumed.
