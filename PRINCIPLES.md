# RSJP Experience System Principles

These principles override convenience, fashion, and short-term optimisation.

1. **Truth over marketing.**  
   Never improve conversion by weakening accuracy.

2. **Students before organisational structure.**  
   Organise the experience around applicant needs, not internal departments.

3. **Trust before persuasion.**  
   Clear facts, conditions, costs, and limitations create stronger recruitment.

4. **Authenticity over decoration.**  
   Real programme experience is the primary brand asset.

5. **Clarity over cleverness.**  
   Users should not need to decode language, navigation, or interactions.

6. **Useful before impressive.**  
   Every section, component, and sentence must earn its place.

7. **Mobile first, not mobile later.**  
   The primary experience must work well on a phone.

8. **Simple before complex.**  
   Use the least complicated implementation that fully solves the current problem.

9. **Working product before speculative architecture.**  
   Do not trade reliable software for unfinished complexity.

10. **Shared facts over duplicated facts.**  
    Programme data should have a clear source of truth.

11. **Accessibility is product quality.**  
    Accessibility is not a final-stage correction.

12. **People before technology.**  
    Technology exists to improve the student and staff experience.

13. **Evidence before assumption.**  
    When facts are uncertain, verify them. Never guess.

14. **Long-term coherence over short-term convenience.**  
    Avoid stopgaps that knowingly create future confusion.

15. **The programme should be memorable; the interface should not compete with it.**
